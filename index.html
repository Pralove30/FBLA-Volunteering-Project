THE SIMPLE VERSION

How it works.

A straightforward way to keep track of the time you give to your community.

01

Show up and lend a hand

Volunteer with a nonprofit, local business, community group, or another cause you care about. Follow their guidance and record the time you actually spend helping.

↗
02

Log your hours

Open Log hours, enter the activity, date, and time spent, then add a short note if it helps you remember the details.

↗
03

Keep your record close

Your entries appear in your dashboard so you can review your activity and total hours. In this starter version, records are stored in your browser only.

↗
04

Need official verification?

This prototype does not contact organizations or verify service. Ask the organization, school, or program that needs your record about its accepted proof and approval process.
